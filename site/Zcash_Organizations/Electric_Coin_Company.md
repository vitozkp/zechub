## Mission Statement

Created by Josh and composed of former ECC members, ZODL was founded with the mission of making financial privacy simple, accessible, and natural for everyone.

At ZODL, the mission has always been to empower economic freedom. Financial privacy is a core part of that vision, essential to protecting human dignity, security, self-sovereignty, and consent.

Obtaining financial privacy should be as easy as closing the curtains in your home. But with the rise of Web2, power has become deeply centralized, and those in control are already inside your living room. You can no longer close the curtains.

That is why we must build something new.

ZODL is creating a new generation of financial tools: accessible to everyone, easy to use, fully private by default, with optional disclosure when needed, and supported by a distributed, resilient, and robust infrastructure.

- 🍎 [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zodl-zcash-wallet/id1672392439)

Built for Private Payments. ZODL is a self-custody shielded ZEC wallet for encrypted digital transactions.

ZODL is the perfect tool for onboarding friends and family into Zcash and private digital payments. Its intuitive design makes it easy for anyone—even those with no prior crypto experience—to set up a wallet, get ZEC, and start transacting in minutes.

### Integrations:

- [Coinbase:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/coinbase-zashi-1-2-release/) crypto newbies can buy ZEC directly within the wallet—skipping the hassles of account registration, KYC, and withdrawing coins from exchanges.

- [Flexa:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zashi-flexa-integration-is-here-spend-zec-at-thousands-of-retailers/) makes it simple to actually use ZEC, allowing new users to spend it at thousands of retail locations like Barnes & Noble, GameStop, and Chipotle.

- [Keystone:](https://electriccoin.co/blog/drumroll-please-introducing-zashi-keystone-hardware-wallet-integration-for-shielded-zec/) a major breakthrough for the Zcash community that has been years in the making. This integration marks a significant evolution in Zcash usability by enabling cold storage of shielded ZEC.

- [NEAR Intents](https://forum.zcashcommunity.com/t/live-in-zashi-decentralized-off-ramp-for-shielded-zec/52034?utm_source=chatgpt.com) The ZODL–NEAR integration lets people use shielded ZEC in ZODL to swap into other supported cryptocurrencies without relying on a centralized exchange. In simple terms, it connects ZODL’s privacy features with NEAR Intents, so users can move from private ZEC to other assets more easily from inside the app.  ￼

## Research and Development

ECC was discontinued, and a significant portion of its developers transitioned to ZODL. However, some of the work was carried out by former ECC members who later departed, as well as by those who are currently part of ZODL.

The team have led recognised breakthroughs on several occasions, the first being the [launch of Zcash](https://electriccoin.co/blog/zcash-begins/) as the worlds first commerical use of Zero-Knowledge Proofs.

Several more following such as the [Sapling network upgrade](https://electriccoin.co/blog/sapling-activation-complete/) allowing for lighter, shielded transactions with a time reduction of 90% for constructing transactions, and a memory reduction of over 97%, enabling for shielded transactions on a mobile device for the first time.

The most significant breakthrough being the most recent. [The creation and deployment](https://electriccoin.co/blog/nu5-activates-on-mainnet-eliminating-trusted-setup-and-launching-a-new-era-for-zcash/) of the halo2 trustless proving system in Network Upgrade 5 for Zcash. This eliminated the trusted setup and launched a new era for Zcash. Since 2022, this has been active on the Zcash Mainnet and allows users to leverage a more secure & easily upgradeable construction for their payment protocol. 

Currently, ZODL’s protocol development efforts are focused on Zallet and the deprecation of zcashd.

## Collaboration & Partnerships 

The ZODL team supports and contributes to the Zcash ecosystem in many ways, collaborating with both internal & external teams regularly to help maintain and improve the Zcash protocol. 

Partnerships include working with Gemini to enable shielded transactions on their platform, Flexa to enable easy Zcash mobile payments at retal locations, hardware wallet companies like Keystone to enable shielded cold storage of ZEC, Brave for Zcash support in the browser, NEAR & other teams in the Zcash ecosystem to bring imrovements to Zcash. 

## Policy & Regulation

ZODLs insight and leadership around on-chain privacy and its implications for personal and national security is incredibly valuable for policy makers. Consistent efforts to appropriately guide policy wherever possible have been made since the ZODL was founded.
